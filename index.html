Not another source-evaluation checklist.

Evidence Lab asks students to choose what to investigate, decide when the evidence is sufficient, identify what mattered most, and live with the consequence of the decision. Sometimes the claim is misleading. Sometimes it is supported. Sometimes the responsible answer is that nobody knows yet.

For librarians and faculty

Library instruction

Use a 15-minute evidence sprint, a 50-minute session, or a full challenge session.

Course integration

Works with composition, first-year seminars, research methods, AI literacy, media studies, and career preparation.

Institution-neutral

Works out of the box. Optional localization is available, but students never need local product knowledge to succeed.
