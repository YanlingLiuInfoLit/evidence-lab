# Evidence Lab

**Follow the evidence. Make the call.**

Evidence Lab is an institution-neutral information-literacy investigation game for college students, created by **Yanling Liu**, Coordinator of Information Literacy and Research Services at Indiana University Kokomo.

Live site: https://yanlingliuinfolit.github.io/evidence-lab/

## Evidence Lab 1.0 experience

Students can choose **2-, 5-, 10-, or 20-minute challenges** or select any of the **36 individual cases**. Challenge times are estimates, not countdowns. Case complexity is organized as Guided, Networked, Contextual, and Synthesis.

## Scoring

Each investigation is worth 10 Investigator Points:

- Decision - 4
- Evidence - 3
- Reasoning - 2
- Investigation Strategy - 1

Confidence is recorded for reflection/calibration and does not affect the score. Students may select multiple pieces of evidence that mattered to their decision. Responsible additional verification is not penalized.

## Investigation Log

Completed investigations from challenges and individually selected cases are stored in the browser using local storage. No sign-in is required. Repeat attempts are retained. Students can print/save a PDF, download CSV, or copy a summary for submission through an LMS. The public edition does not automatically transmit student activity. Clearing browser/site data can remove the log.

## For educators

The repository includes a spoiler-free Teaching Guide and ready-to-use 2-, 5-, 10-, and 20-minute challenge lesson plans, a choose-your-own-cases assignment, and an asynchronous Investigation Log assignment.

A separate confidential Instructor Key contains spoilers and is intentionally excluded from this public repository.
