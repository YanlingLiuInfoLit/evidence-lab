Student submission workflow

  1. Assign a challenge length or specific cases.
  2. Students work without signing in; completed attempts are stored in that browser.
  3. Students open Investigation Log and use Print / Save PDF, Download CSV, or Copy Summary.
  4. Students submit the file or summary through your existing LMS.

The public edition does not automatically transmit student work. Browser-local records are appropriate for formative/low-stakes use, not secure high-stakes assessment.

ACRL Framework alignment

Evidence Lab is informed by and aligned with the ACRL Framework for Information Literacy for Higher Education (2026 revision). It is not intended as a comprehensive assessment of every Framework knowledge practice or disposition. See the Teaching Guide for the six-frame mapping.
