Cite & Reuse Evidence Lab

Use it. Adapt it. Teach with it. Give credit.

Creator

Yanling Liu
Coordinator of Information Literacy and Research Services
Indiana University Kokomo

Evidence Lab is an institution-neutral information-literacy investigation game designed to help college students practice evaluating evidence, tracing information, calibrating uncertainty, and making responsible information decisions.

The views and design of Evidence Lab are those of the creator and do not necessarily represent Indiana University policy or endorsement.

Visual-artifact disclosure

The simulated visual artifacts used in Cases 13–16 were created specifically for Evidence Lab using generative AI. They are instructional simulations rather than documentary photographs and are included for educational reuse with the project.

Adapting Evidence Lab

Educators are welcome to adapt cases, translate materials, localize selected institutional terminology, create new teaching activities, or build on the software. Please preserve the distinction between simulated content and real institutional policies, and do not make local product familiarity necessary to solve an investigation.
