About Evidence Lab

Evidence Lab is an institution-neutral information-literacy investigation game for college students.

Creator

Yanling Liu, Coordinator of Information Literacy and Research Services, Indiana University Kokomo.

Evidence Lab was designed to help students practice evaluating evidence, tracing information, calibrating uncertainty, and making responsible information decisions across research, AI, social media, news, visual media, careers, and everyday life.

The views and design of Evidence Lab are those of the creator and do not necessarily represent institutional policy or endorsement.

Design philosophy

The game does not teach “be skeptical of everything.” Investigation may confirm information, qualify it, reject it, or preserve uncertainty. Students are rewarded for high-value evidence and proportionate stopping—not for clicking everything.

Validation status

This public edition has undergone extensive internal design, calibration, portability, and accessibility-oriented review. It has not yet been claimed as validated through a real-student research study.
